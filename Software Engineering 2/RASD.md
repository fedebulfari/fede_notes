# RASD

The purpose of RASD(_Requirements Analysis and Specification Document_) is to **Communicate** an understanding of the requirements, making a **Contractual** binding with the customers, defining a **Baseline** for other acivities as project planning, software verification and validation, change control

Audience:
- Customers and Users
- System Analysts and Requirement Analysts
- Developers and Programmers
- Quality Assurance teams
- Project Managers

 ![[Requirement Engineering-1792747110337.webp|439x175]]
 >
> Templates could be:
> $$\text{[Subject][Action][Constraint of Action]}\\ or \\ \text{[Condition][Subject][Action][Object][Constraint of Action]}$$

## 1. Target Qualities

- Completeness
- Precision
- Pertinence
- Consistency
- Unambiguity
- Feasibility
- Comprehensibility
- Good Structuring
- Modifiability
- Traceability
