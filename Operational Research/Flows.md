## 1. Maximum Flow Problem

Given a direct graph $G=(N,A)$ and two nodes $s,t ∈ N$ integer) 
Capacities(integer): $u_{ij} ≥ 0, ∀(i, j) ∈ A$
Send as much flow as possible from $s$ to $t$ 
Constraints: arc capacities flow conservation atintermediate nodes
